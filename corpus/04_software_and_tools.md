# Software & Tools

## Core Software Stack
- Tax preparation: ProTax Suite
- Bookkeeping and write-up: LedgerPro
- Document management: VaultDMS
- Secure client portal and e-signature: ClientBridge
- Time tracking and billing: TimeKeeper

## Getting Access
Request access to any system through the office manager. New-hire accounts are
provisioned within one business day of your start date. Access to a specific
client's files is granted only after you are assigned to that engagement.

## Password and Login Policy
All firm systems require multi-factor authentication. Passwords must be at least
14 characters and are changed every 90 days. Never reuse a firm password on an
outside service. Use the firm password manager for all credentials.

## Support
For software problems, first check the internal FAQ. If unresolved, contact the
office manager, who coordinates with each vendor's support line. Do not contact
vendors directly with client data.
