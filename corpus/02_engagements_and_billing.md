# Engagements & Billing

## Engagement Letters
No client work begins until a signed engagement letter is on file. The letter
defines the scope, the fee basis (fixed-fee or hourly), and the period covered.
Any change in scope requires a written addendum before the additional work
starts. Engagement letters are renewed annually each January.

## Standard Hourly Rates
Our standard rates, reviewed every January, are:
- Partner: $325 per hour
- Senior Accountant: $185 per hour
- Staff Accountant: $120 per hour
- Bookkeeping: $85 per hour
Fixed-fee engagements are quoted from these rates plus an estimate of hours.

## Invoicing and Payment
Invoices are issued on the first business day of each month for the prior
month's work. Payment terms are net 15. A reminder is sent at 30 days; balances
over 60 days are escalated to the engagement partner before any further work is
performed. Clients may pay by ACH through the secure portal or by check.

## Write-offs and Discounts
Any fee write-off or discount over $250 requires partner approval and must be
documented in the client file with a short reason.
