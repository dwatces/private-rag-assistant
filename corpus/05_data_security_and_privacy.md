# Data Security & Privacy

## Client Data Handling
All client data stays within firm-controlled systems. Never email tax documents
or Social Security numbers as unencrypted attachments. Share files only through
the secure ClientBridge portal. Paper documents are scanned into VaultDMS and
the originals returned to the client or shredded.

## Private AI Policy
The firm permits AI tools only when client data stays on firm-controlled
infrastructure. Client information must never be pasted into public, third-party
cloud AI services. Our internal knowledge assistant runs entirely on firm
hardware for this reason — no client or firm data leaves the building.

## Access Control
Staff may access only the client files for engagements they are assigned to.
Access is reviewed quarterly and revoked immediately when an engagement ends or
a staff member departs.

## Breach Response
If you suspect any data has been exposed — a lost laptop, a misdirected email, a
suspicious login — report it to the office manager and managing partner within
one hour. Do not attempt to investigate alone. The firm follows a written
incident-response plan and notifies affected clients as required by law.
