# Internal FAQ

## Paid Time Off
Staff accrue 15 days of paid time off per year, plus firm holidays. Submit
requests at least two weeks in advance through TimeKeeper. PTO is not approved
during the two weeks before a major filing deadline except in emergencies.

## Expense Reimbursement
Submit receipts through the VaultDMS expense module within 30 days of the
expense. Approved reimbursements are paid with the next payroll run. Mileage is
reimbursed at the standard firm rate set each January.

## Continuing Education
Each professional staff member must complete their required CPE hours by
December 31. The firm covers the cost of approved courses; submit the course for
approval before registering.

## Who to Ask
- System or software access: office manager
- PTO, payroll, benefits: office manager
- Technical tax questions: your engagement's senior reviewer
- Client billing disputes: the engagement partner
- Anything involving possible data exposure: office manager and managing partner
