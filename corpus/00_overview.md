# Birchwood & Vale, CPAs — Internal Operations Handbook

## Purpose
This handbook is the single source of truth for how our firm operates day to
day. It covers client onboarding, engagements and billing, our tax-season
workflow, the software we use, data-security rules, and answers to common staff
questions. When a policy here conflicts with something you were told verbally,
the handbook wins until it is formally updated.

## How to Use the Knowledge Assistant
Rather than searching through documents, staff can ask the firm assistant a
question in plain English — for example, "what is our onboarding process for a
new business client?" or "what are our standard billing rates?". The assistant
answers using only the contents of this handbook and shows you which section
each answer came from, so you can verify it. If the answer is not in the
handbook, the assistant will say so rather than guess.

## Keeping It Current
The office manager reviews the handbook every quarter. If you find something
out of date or missing, send a note to the office manager and it will be added
in the next review cycle.
