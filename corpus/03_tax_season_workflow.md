# Tax Season Workflow

## Internal Deadlines
For individual returns, the preparer must deliver a complete draft to the
reviewer no later than ten business days before the filing deadline. Returns
received from clients within two weeks of the deadline are automatically placed
on extension to protect quality and avoid rushed work.

## Two-Tier Review
Every return goes through two-tier review before filing:
1. Preparer completes the return and a self-review checklist.
2. A senior reviewer checks the return against source documents and prior year.
3. For returns with a balance due over $25,000 or any new business entity, a
   partner provides final sign-off.
Nothing is e-filed until the reviewer has marked the return "approved to file."

## Extensions
If a return cannot be completed accurately by the deadline, file an extension
before the deadline, record it in the practice-management system, and notify the
client the same day with any estimated payment they should make.

## Client Communication During Season
Clients receive a status update at intake, at "in review," and at "ready to
file." Questions to clients are batched where possible to avoid repeated
back-and-forth.
