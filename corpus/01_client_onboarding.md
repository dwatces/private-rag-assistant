# Client Onboarding

## New Client Intake Process
Every new client follows the same five-step intake:
1. Send the welcome packet and engagement letter for signature.
2. Collect the prior two years of tax returns, government-issued ID, and any
   relevant entity documents (articles of incorporation, EIN letter).
3. Set the client up in our practice-management system and secure portal.
4. Assign a lead accountant who owns the relationship end to end.
5. Schedule a kickoff call within five business days of the signed letter.

## Required Documents by Client Type
- Individuals: prior-year 1040, ID, and a completed intake questionnaire.
- Businesses: prior-year business return, formation documents, EIN letter,
  prior-year financial statements, and a list of authorized contacts.
- Nonprofits: determination letter, prior Form 990, and board contact list.

## Who Owns Onboarding
The assigned lead accountant is responsible for completing the onboarding
checklist. The office manager provisions system and portal access. Onboarding
is considered complete only when the kickoff call has happened and all required
documents are stored in the secure portal.
